How to Plan a Halloween Birthday Party: A Complete Checklist

By the PartyHard team · 26 November 2026

Got a birthday in late October or November? A Halloween birthday party lets you combine two celebrations into one unforgettable night. Use this checklist to plan it.

2 weeks before

  • Fix the date, the guest list and the venue (home, rooftop or a party hall).
  • Send invites with a costume rule, for example “come as your favourite villain”.
  • Decide on a spooky level: “cute and fun” for families, “properly scary” for adults.

1 week before

  • Decor: black and orange balloons, cobwebs, fairy lights, carved pumpkins and a fog machine.
  • Photo booth: a haunted backdrop with props such as masks, witch hats and fake spiders.
  • Cake: order a themed cake, such as a glow-in-the-dark or a “bleeding” red velvet design.

Food and drinks

  • “Mummy” paneer rolls, “witch finger” breadsticks and pumpkin soup shots.
  • A mocktail bar with dry-ice “potions”.

Games and activities

  • A costume contest with a prize for the scariest look.
  • A treasure hunt in the dark with torches.
  • A horror-story circle with the lights off.

On the day

  • Set up decor at least 3 hours before guests arrive.
  • Test the fog machine and lights; keep exits clear and lit for safety.
  • Plan the cake moment for the peak of the party.
